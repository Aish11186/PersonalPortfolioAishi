Fillit: The autofill extension

>Built a lightweight Chrome browser extension with Plasmo that makes repetitive autofilling easier for fields where the browser's native autofill doesn't reliably learn — especially LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio links, and cold-email workflows.

>Supports custom autofill fields for LinkedIn, GitHub, and Portfolio, with inline autocomplete that detects trigger prefixes like `portfolio:`, `linkedin:`, or `github:` and offers your saved value as a suggestion.

>Designed to be cold-email friendly — quickly insert your portfolio, LinkedIn, or GitHub while writing emails without switching tabs to copy links.

>Lets you configure your details once through the extension's options page, and your saved information becomes available across any website where you need it.
